      Quick start

      No tech degree required.

      Here's everything you need to know.

      How do I make a prompt?

      Tap Generator. Choose a category and task, enter your topic, then hit Generate My Prompt. Copy the result into your AI tool.

      What does Surprise Me do?

      It selects a random category, task, audience, tone, format, and detail level. If you entered a topic, it keeps that topic unless you intentionally clear it. Use the lock buttons to protect other selections.

      Where are my saved prompts?

      In Saved. Your prompts are stored locally in this browser, not online. Clearing site data or switching devices can remove them. Copy important prompts somewhere safe too.

      Does this generator create AI images or run prompts?

      No. It writes detailed text prompts for you to use in other AI tools. It does not connect to a paid model or generate images itself.

      Can I customize the generated result?

      Yes. Add requirements, background details, what to avoid, and special instructions. You can also copy the output into a document and edit it further.
